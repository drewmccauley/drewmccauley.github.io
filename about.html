About Me

I am a Senior at Marist University pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance and a minor in Accounting.

My academic journey has been shaped not only by coursework but also by real-world leadership and global experiences. At Marist, I’ve served in student leadership positions such as Treasurer and Vice President of the Business Club and President of the Residence Hall Council, where I learned the importance of communication, delegation, and community engagement. In Spring 2025, I studied abroad in Dublin, Ireland, where I expanded my perspective on international business and gained professional experience through an internship with Multiple Sclerosis Ireland. This opportunity deepened my passion for finance, problem-solving, and making an impact in diverse environments. Beyond academics and work, I am passionate about continuous learning, networking, and professional development to build meaningful connections and broaden my skill set.
