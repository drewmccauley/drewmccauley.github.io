About Me

As a finance student at Marist University, I've built my foundation through both classroom rigor and hands-on experience. My leadership roles—from managing budgets as Business Club Treasurer to representing 200+ residents as Residence Hall Council President—taught me that effective finance requires both analytical precision and clear communication. My internships at Sompo and Screenvision Media gave me direct exposure to the work I'm pursuing: financial analysis, risk assessment, and sales. Studying abroad in Dublin deepened this experience through coursework in EU political economy and an advocacy internship at Multiple Sclerosis Ireland, where I applied financial controls and data governance in a mission-driven setting. I'm particularly drawn to roles where rigorous analysis meets real impact—financial analysis, underwriting, auditing, and risk assessment. I'm seeking opportunities where I can apply quantitative skills to evaluate risk, strengthen controls, and support strategic decision-making.
