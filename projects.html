Projects

Investment Analysis Portfolio Project

I constructed and analyzed a moderately conservative three-security portfolio consisting of two equities and one mutual fund over a full semester. I performed comprehensive financial statement analysis, three-year ratio analysis across nine key metrics, and developed financial projections for the upcoming fiscal year using geometric growth rates to forecast revenues, margins, and funding needs. I leveraged the Bloomberg Terminal to gather market data, track insider transactions, analyze beneficial ownership structures, and monitor analyst recommendations. I utilized Excel for financial modeling, daily performance tracking, and calculating portfolio returns using CAPM and beta analysis. I evaluated investment decisions against market benchmarks, assessed management teams for potential conflicts, and analyzed sector positioning through heat maps and peer comparisons.

L'Oréal x Avatar: Fire and Ash Strategic Partnership Analysis

I collaborated with a team of three interns to develop a strategic partnership proposal between L'Oréal and the upcoming Avatar: Fire and Ash film release, leveraging Zoe Saldana's role as both the film's star and L'Oréal brand ambassador. The campaign concept centered on cinema advertisements featuring Saldana showcasing makeup products connected to the film's distinctive character aesthetics. I conducted demographic research comparing L'Oréal's target audience with Avatar's moviegoer profile and analyzed cinema advertising effectiveness data to demonstrate campaign potential. I also researched box office projections for Avatar 3 to assess partnership value and reach. I contributed to presentation development and presented findings to Screenvision leadership, receiving positive feedback on the strategic alignment and market analysis supporting the partnership opportunity.

Internal Audit Process Improvement Initiative

I developed and presented a comprehensive review of audit work to senior leadership, summarizing key findings from supporting financial controls audits (including business combinations, financial accounting, and accounts payable) as well as operational audits (such as financial crime compliance). I identified a critical inefficiency in the communication process between internal audit and other departments, where repeated document requests caused significant time delays. I proposed implementing a centralized software system allowing the audit team to submit requests, automatically notify relevant departments, and enable seamless file sharing. The solution aimed to improve response times, enhance document tracking, and reduce coordination friction. I presented the proposal to senior audit leadership, receiving positive feedback on the approach to improving cross-functional collaboration and workflow efficiency.
